01

Introduction

PlanScale is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

02

Information We Collect

Photos, Images & Project Data

We collect photos, annotations, tags, measurements, and project notes you capture or upload within the app. This data is stored locally on your device and is not shared with third parties unless you explicitly export or share it.

Location Data

We collect approximate and precise location data to:

  • Tag projects with location information
  • Automatically fill address fields
  • Enhance project organisation

Location data is stored locally and included in reports only when you choose to export or share them.

Account Information (Email)

We collect your email address for authentication through Supabase (email OTP login). This is required to secure trial eligibility and subscription usage limits across reinstalls.

Subscription & Usage Data

We store subscription state and usage limits in Supabase to prevent abuse and enforce limits.

Purchase History

We collect purchase and subscription information through RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases. This information is used solely for payment processing, subscription management, fraud prevention, and support.

03

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Authenticate accounts and secure trial/limit enforcement
  • Process in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Generate project reports and documentation
  • Improve user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
04

Data Storage & Security

  • Photos and project data are stored locally on your device
  • Account, subscription, and usage data are stored securely on Supabase
  • All network communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • We implement industry-standard security measures throughout
